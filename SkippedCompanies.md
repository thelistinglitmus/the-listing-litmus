# The Listing Litmus — Skipped Companies

Companies on this list have failed the [vetting checklist](VETTING.md) on at least three independent third-party signals. Skip on sight, do not engage.

**Last updated**: 2026-05-21 (4 entries)

## Why a company lands here

One or more of:
- Confirmed scam / AI-data-harvesting / resume-mining pattern
- Unfinished or fake-looking corporate website (lorem ipsum, placeholder addresses, non-assigned area codes, copyright contradictions)
- Bulk posting under varied titles from the same firm with no real hires (whack-a-mole pattern)
- Scam-Detector trust score below 30/100 with phishing or spamming flags
- Independent third-party scam reports (Reddit, blog writeups, YouTube callouts)
- Pre-recorded bot "interviews" that funnel applicant responses into AI training data

## Crossing Hurdles
*Also known as: crossinghurdles.com*

- **Added**: 2026-05-21
- **Pattern**: Confirmed AI-data-mining operation. Funnels applicants into pre-recorded bot "interviews" framed as job assessments, harvests responses to train AI models. No real hires reported.
- **Observable facts**: 4,946 active LinkedIn listings against 11-50 employees and 4 million followers. That ratio is structurally impossible for legitimate recruitment at that headcount. Reposts the same role under varied titles (AD Specialist, AD Engineer, Systems Engineer TypeScript/Rust, SRE $70/hr, Platform Engineer $70-150/hr, Notion Specialist $20-70/hr, RunOps Support Engineer $25-50/hr). All Promoted (paid) placements on LinkedIn.
- **Founder**: Sankalp Chhabra (India-based)
- **Sources**:
  - [Scam Detector trust score 12.8/100 (flags phishing/spamming)](https://www.scam-detector.com/validator/crossinghurdles-com-review/)
  - [Colliepixels: CrossingHurdles.com Under Fire — Fake Jobs & AI Data Harvesting Scheme](https://www.colliepixels.com/post/crossinghurdles-com-under-fire-fake-jobs-ai-data-harvesting-scheme)
  - Reddit r/recruitinghell pattern reports
  - Glassdoor: candidates note "can't find anyone who actually got a job through this company"
- **What to watch for in their listings**: Promoted (paid) LinkedIn placement, $/hr ranges that span suspiciously wide ($25-50, $20-70, $70-150), "Actively reviewing applicants" badge, titles you've never seen them post before suddenly appearing alongside their usual fare.

## Tek-Staffing / TekNavigators Staffing LLC
*Also known as: tek-staffing.com*

- **Added**: 2026-05-18
- **Pattern**: US-fronted website with the actual operation in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India. Resume-harvesting shop pattern, risk of submitting your resume to roles without right-to-represent agreement (blocks legitimate recruiters from representing you later).
- **Observable facts (direct site inspection)**:
  - Lorem ipsum text on two service boxes of the live production site
  - Header address reads "Chicago 12, Melborne City, USA" (Melbourne is not in Chicago; not in the USA)
  - Footer real address: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India
  - US phone (640) 333-0340 uses an area code not assigned by NANPA
  - Second phone number `+88 0168...` uses Bangladesh country code
  - Domain `tek-staffing.com` doesn't match LLC name "TekNavigators Staffing LLC"
  - Copyright 2023 but service blurb claims "8 years of expertise"
  - All five social media icons link to "#" (nowhere)
- **Sources**:
  - Direct site inspection 2026-05-18 (multiple address and contact contradictions on live page)
- **What to watch for in their listings**: Generic IT staffing roles cross-posted to LinkedIn, India-headquartered staffing firm with US-fronting website pattern, "Aerospace / Automotive / Medical / Industrial Automation" all-of-the-above specialty claims.

## Yochana IT Solutions
*Also known as: yochana.com*

- **Added**: 2026-05-21
- **Pattern**: Documented resume-harvesting recruiter shop. BBB and Glassdoor describe the pattern explicitly: cold calls about "great openings," 8+ spam emails per day for positions inappropriate for the applicant's experience, no actual placement.
- **Observable facts**: Promoted LinkedIn listings for Windows/Linux sysadmin roles with Easy Apply. "Company review time typically 1 week" framing on LinkedIn cards. Hyderabad IN office presence.
- **Sources**:
  - [Glassdoor reviews (4.0 / 42 with disputed company-defends-fake-reviews pattern)](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Yochana-IT-Solutions-Reviews-E1000990.htm) — including the verbatim "Fake. They will call you for great openings for you, just to get hand on your resume. dont ever send them your resume"
  - [BBB profile](https://www.bbb.org/us/mi/farmington/profile/technical-recruiting/yochana-it-solutions-inc-0372-90028440/customer-reviews) — including the verbatim "the title is a scam" and complaint of 8+ spam emails per day for irrelevant positions
  - [Indeed reviews](https://www.indeed.com/cmp/Yochana-IT-Solutions/reviews)
- **What to watch for in their listings**: Generic Windows/Linux sysadmin Easy Apply roles, "Company review time typically 1 week" template on the listing card, Yochana logo (Y in red diamond).

## Talentoma
*Also known as: talentoma.com*

- **Added**: 2026-05-21
- **Pattern**: Recently-registered domain (a high red flag for scam shops). Atypical compensation format. Thin company information across all sources.
- **Observable facts**:
  - Scam Detector trust score 51.7/100 (medium-risk, flagged for phishing/spamming)
  - Scamdoc trust score 25%
  - Posts "Remote IT Support Assistant" roles at $6,100-$7,000 PER MONTH — atypical for the advertised role (support roles are usually salaried /yr or hourly /hr, not /month, which is closer to a contractor day-rate or MLM monthly target framing)
  - Easy Apply + Promoted (paid) LinkedIn placement
  - Generic role descriptions, thin company information
- **Sources**:
  - [Scam Detector talentoma.com review](https://www.scam-detector.com/validator/talentoma-com-review/)
  - [Scamdoc talentoma.com](https://www.scamdoc.com/view/2383001)
- **What to watch for in their listings**: $/month pay framing on what should be $/hr or $/yr roles, "Remote IT Support Assistant" or similar entry-level titles, Easy Apply + Promoted badge.
