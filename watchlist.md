Capture full company identifiers on next sighting and re-vet.

## "Jobs via Dice" (LinkedIn aggregation tag)
- **First seen**: 2026-05-21 (multiple FT listings via this tag)
- **Status**: NOT a scam tag. Dice is a legitimate (if low-quality) tech job board syndicating its listings to LinkedIn. "Jobs via Dice" listings are real Dice listings, not fake. The phishing problem is bad actors impersonating Dice recruiters via Telegram or WhatsApp — a separate concern outside this list's scope.
- **Practical note**: Treat the "Jobs via Dice" tag as a low-signal source (often W2 staffing arbitrage, federal contractor staffing, or recruiter middlemen). Don't auto-block. Discount priority on identical-pay generic-title listings that share this tag.
